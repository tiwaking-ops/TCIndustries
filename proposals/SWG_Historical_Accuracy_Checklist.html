Authority and non-authority — read before recording anything

This page is a local transport tool. It carries no project authority of any kind. It is a fill-and-export sheet, not a decision record, not a ruling, and not a source of status.

Every verdict on this page is yours. Nothing here is pre-filled, and this tool will not compute one for you. A blank row stays blank in the export — it is never written as a match.

A completed run produces EVIDENCE, not decisions. It closes no open question, changes no status, authorises no tuning, and sets no target. Per BAL-001 and HD-TST-01, no numeric change and no mechanism change may follow from it without separate authorisation.

No composite score. The tally shown below is a count of recorded outcomes. It must not be converted into a score, a percentage, or a pass mark.

Observe, never steer. A divergence is recorded as a finding. Fixes are not proposed here — they require a separate, authorised proposal.

Execution rules (instrument §2)

  • Every unfilled row stays unfilled. INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE and NOT OBSERVABLE HERE are correct, valuable answers.
  • INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE is not a soft MATCH. An affirmative one-word answer is weak evidence of satisfaction and near-zero evidence of mechanism (record §7 item 6).
  • NOT OBSERVABLE HERE is not the same thing. It means the account is fine and the arena is not — e.g. a mechanic not built, or no live cohort.
  • Record raw counts beside every percentage. A 40/42 cohort is 95%, not a verdict.
  • Recollected magnitudes are never targets. Any figure in the Pre-CU column is a recalled magnitude, quoted as such and nothing else.
  • Mode C (live cohort) is where Part 2 actually lives. Modes A and B do not clear it. On a small cohort, a Mode B DIVERGES may be a cohort artefact — record the cohort.
  • THIN and ABSENT rows are marked. On a THIN row a MATCH is near-meaningless; on an ABSENT row, INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE is the expected verdict.
  • RULED SURFACE rows are marked. A DIVERGES there is a re-opening question for the owner, not a status change. The ruling stands until the owner moves it.
  • Do not edit the Pre-CU column. It is a quotation of a filed record. If it is wrong, that is a finding about the record, filed separately.
Verdicts, observations and notes autosave to this browser as you type. Nothing leaves your machine. The form has no network calls.
