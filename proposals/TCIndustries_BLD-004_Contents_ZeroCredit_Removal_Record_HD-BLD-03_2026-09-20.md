## 1. The ruling (as decided by the owner)

|| # | Question | Owner decision |

|| 1 | What happens to a building's contents when maintenance credit reaches 0? | **Everything in the building is also removed from the world.** Items, vendors, and everything else inside — all removed with the structure. No salvage. No recovery window. No restitution. |

### Decision

1. **CONTENTS REMOVED WITH STRUCTURE:** when a building's maintenance credit
   reaches 0, everything in the building is also removed from the world —
   items, vendors, and everything else inside. No salvage. No recovery window.
   No restitution.
2. **UPKEEP PERIOD:** the upkeep lapses after a 24-hour period of zero
   maintenance credit (owner-authorized value; historically grounded in Pre-CU
   SWG building-maintenance behaviour). At the end of that period, the building
   and its contents are removed.
3. **REASON:** simplest system; historically accurate to Pre-CU SWG. Acknowledged
   as a brutal rule — the worst hit falls on guilds: if a guild does not pay
   upkeep on its guild hall, it loses everything in the hall unless it removes
   everything beforehand, including all vendors if any are present.
