**One-page summary. This document creates no design authority.** Every statement below is cited to the ruling that establishes it, in `TCIndustries_Human_Rulings_Register.md`. Full evidentiary detail (why each finding was reached) lives in the archived EIC evidence chain, not here.

## Headline

> **Architecture C — the leading candidate for the Economic Interdependence Core — is RETIRED.**
> No EIC architecture currently holds leading-hypothesis status.
> No further EIC architectural work is authorised until the human supplies a specific structural-boundary statement.
> *(HD-EIC-05, HD-EIC-07)*

## What Is Settled (HUMAN-LOCKED)

- **The interdependence requirement itself is unchanged.** PIL-003 ("important professions should depend upon one another... interdependence must create opportunity rather than coercive inconvenience") remains binding and was explicitly *not* weakened by Architecture C's failure. *(HD-EIC-06)*
- **The unit of validation is layered:** character specialisation is a desirable identity/progression property, but the decisive economic test is comparative advantage at the independent-participant layer (including multi-account and organisational actors), not merely at the single-character layer. *(HD-EIC-01)*
- **Specialists must retain meaningful comparative advantage without relying on a hard factory-quality ceiling.** No such ceiling is authorised. *(HD-EIC-02)*
- **Legitimate multi-accounting is an in-scope adversarial condition.** No account-level restriction is authorised. *(HD-EIC-03)*
- **Architecture C failed this test.** Every tested coherent package (α, β, γ′-1, γ′-2) failed full adversarial closure — no residual channel survived combined manufacturing-quality parity, multi-account internalisation, organisational pooling, NPC substitution, and demand neutralisation, applied simultaneously.

## The Single Outstanding Gate

**HD-EIC-07 requires the human to state which structural source(s) of economic comparative advantage the project is willing to make a durable design commitment to**, before any bounded new architectural shaping can begin. Examples of the *form* such a statement might take (illustrative only, not proposals — taken directly from the ruling document):
- Explicit acceptance that certain forms of scarcity, information asymmetry, or demand differentiation will be treated as durable design commitments.
- Explicit rejection of pure soft-residual approaches that organisations can fully internalise.
- Any other human-defined constraint that bounds the next search.

**Until this statement exists, the programme remains at the post-falsification gate.** The human may also choose to pause indefinitely and supply boundaries later; that is an equally valid outcome under HD-EIC-07 and does not require further action.
