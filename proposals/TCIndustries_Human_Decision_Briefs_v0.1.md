## 1. Objective & Scope

Task T-08 provides structured **Decision-Support Briefs** for the four major design areas in TCIndustries that are pure human design decisions rather than simulation outputs (GDD §36, Open Questions Register §4):
1. **Currency & Money Sinks (OQ-009)**
2. **Combat Scope & Risk Model (OQ-008)**
3. **Transportation & Logistics Model (OQ-006)**
4. **City Civic Depth & Governance (OQ-007 follow-up)**

Each brief outlines: the core decision, the LOCKED constraints bounding it, candidate options with trade-offs, existing evidence, and the minimal discrimination criteria.

---

## Brief 1 — Currency & Money Sinks (OQ-009)

- **Status & Existing Ruling:** Structure A (single unified currency) and INV-009a (faucet-exceeds-sink mandate) were ruled under **HD-ECO-01**. Sink instrument classes and numeric magnitudes (BAL-001) remain open.
- **Bounding Constraints:** HD-ECO-01 faucet mandate; HD-PROF-03 training cost affordability; HD-BLD-02 maintenance credit model.
- **Candidate Option Space for Sink Classes:**
  * *Option 1 — Consumable Civic Upkeep & Maintenance:* Structure maintenance fees (HD-BLD-02) and city specialisation maintenance.
  * *Option 2 — Training & Certification Fees:* Credit-gated profession training (HD-PROF-03).
  * *Option 3 — Decay/Loss Sinks:* Item repair and death penalty sinks (HD-ITM-01/02).
- **Discrimination Criterion:** Do sinks scale proportionally with money creation without choking early-game on-ramps (backlog 6c)?

## Brief 2 — Combat Scope & Risk Model (OQ-008)

- **Status:** TBD (Open Questions Register §2). Testbed combat evidence (Phases 0–3) is PROTOTYPE/HISTORICAL only.
- **Bounding Constraints:** PIL-003 (Interdependence); HD-SCOPE-01 (S1 Economic Spine focus; combat deferred-TBD).
- **Candidate Option Space:**
  * *Option 1 — PvE + Factional PvP (Galactic Civil War per HD-GCW-01):* Ground-based factional warfare, overt/covert states, base attacks.
  * *Option 2 — Open PvP with Full Loot / Partial Loss:* High-risk sandbox combat.
  * *Option 3 — PvE-Centric Sandbox:* Combat as creature control and mission execution, zero mandatory PvP.
- **Discrimination Criterion:** Alignment with the S1 economic spine and the non-combat career demand requirement (AS-005).

## Brief 3 — Transportation & Logistics Model (OQ-006)

- **Status:** TBD (Open Questions Register §2). Zero evidence in corpus (Evidence Register gap 1).
- **Bounding Constraints:** WRLD-001 (Persistent Shared World); WRLD-005; economic regional differentiation (AS-003).
- **Candidate Option Space:**
  * *Option 1 — Real-Time Physical Travel (Mounts, Vehicles, Shuttles):* High transit friction, creating strong demand for regional crafting and local merchant positioning.
  * *Option 2 — Fast Travel / Instant Teleportation:* Low friction, flattening regional economic differentiation.
  * *Option 3 — Player-Operated Transit Infrastructure:* Transit networks built, fueled, and tolled by player organisations.
- **Discrimination Criterion:** Whether transport friction preserves regional economic differentiation (AS-003) without violating PIL-003 ("coercive inconvenience").

## Brief 4 — City Civic Depth & Governance (OQ-007 Follow-Up)

- **Status:** Partially ruled by **HD-BLD-01** (free-claim formation + basic maintenance depth). Civic depth (policies, treasuries, municipal offices, citizenship rights) remains open.
- **Bounding Constraints:** BLD-004 abandonment rules (HD-BLD-02); SAFE-001 property safeguards.
- **Candidate Option Space:**
  * *Option 1 — Minimalist Zoning & Maintenance:* Mayors collect taxes and maintain structures; no complex political legislation.
  * *Option 2 — Full Civic Legislature & Taxation:* Elected mayors, municipal treasuries, zoning laws, commercial tax rates, and structured citizenship rights.
- **Discrimination Criterion:** Whether civic depth generates genuine emergent player society without imposing unmanageable administrative overhead on participants.
