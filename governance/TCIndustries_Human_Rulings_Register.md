# TCIndustries — Human Rulings Register

**Purpose of this document:** A single, chronologically ordered, cumulative record of every explicit human ruling issued for the TCIndustries project to date, across both the EIC programme and GDD-level authority questions. This document **merges** the content of three source rulings — two EIC ruling documents (Section A) and one recovered-and-newly-recorded GDD ruling (Section B) — for single-document readability.

**Authority:** This register carries the same HUMAN-LOCKED authority as its source rulings, to the exact extent stated in each entry below — no more, no less. Nothing in this register expands, reinterprets, or extends any ruling beyond what its source states.

**Provenance guarantee:** The two EIC-programme source documents remain preserved, verbatim and unedited, in archive (`/archive/human_rulings_source/`) as the permanent source of truth. **On any conflict between this register and an original ruling document, the original document governs.** Section B (HD-GDD-01) has no equivalent standalone source document — see its provenance note — so for that ruling, **this register is itself the primary record.**

**Governance note (restated identically from both EIC source documents):** Per GR-001 and project authority rules, these statements are HUMAN-LOCKED only to the extent of the decisions stated below. Downstream mechanical implementations remain PROPOSED or TBD unless and until separately ruled. Canonical presence in this register does not itself constitute human approval of anything beyond what is explicitly recorded.

**Ruling Context Disclosure (per GR-004 — see `Manifest.md` for full text):** Every ruling below now carries a `Ruling context` line stating which documents the producing LLM session had available. Where the original source document already lists a "Controlling References" section, that is used as the best available proxy and is noted as such — it was not originally collected for this purpose.

## Part I — HD-EIC-01 to HD-EIC-04
**Source:** `EIC_Human_Rulings_2026-08-25.md` · **Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Responds to:** `EIC_Human_Decision_Brief_v0.1.md` (Gates 1–4)
**Ruling context (per GR-004; reconstructed from the source document's own "Controlling prior documents"):** `EIC_Human_Decision_Brief_v0.1.md`, `EIC_Candidate_v0.1.md`, `EIC_Minimum_Simulation_Results_v0.1.md`, `Master_GDD_v1.1.1_Status_Patch.md`, `Authority_Provenance_Reconciliation_Matrix.md`. *(Proxy only — not originally collected as a context-disclosure statement; see note above.)*

### HD-EIC-01 — Layered Unit of Interdependence
**Decision: APPROVE D — Layered.**
Character specialisation remains an intended character-level design property. EIC validation ultimately concerns economically meaningful comparative advantage among independent economic participants, including legitimate multi-account and organisational actors.
**Explicitly does not determine:** the implementation of specialisation (formal budget, soft pressure, or other instruments).
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED (unit of validation only). **Implementation status:** Open / PROPOSED alternatives remain available.

### HD-EIC-02 — Specialist Comparative Value
**Decision: APPROVE hierarchical comparative-value principle.**
Specialists must retain economically meaningful comparative advantages arising from expertise, information, experimentation, discovery, responsiveness, customisation, production economics, reputation, provenance, or related player-created value.
**Explicitly does not authorise:** any permanent hard factory-quality ceiling.
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED (principle only). **Implementation status:** PROPOSED / TBD; no specific residual channel is elevated to LOCKED.

### HD-EIC-03 — Multi-Accounting
**Decision: APPROVE M1.**
Legitimate multi-accounting is an in-scope adversarial condition for EIC validation. The system is not required to equalise multi-account and independent-player economics, but multi-account internalisation must not trivially eliminate economically meaningful independent participation.
**Explicitly does not authorise:** any account-level restriction.
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED (validation stance only). **Implementation status:** Open.

### HD-EIC-04 — Functional Shapes
**Decision: APPROVE.**
The EIC team may define provisional non-numeric functional shapes necessary for simulation. These remain PROPOSED / simulation assumptions, not canonical mechanics.
**Explicitly does not authorise:** any numeric balance values.
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED (methodological permission only).

**Cumulative effect of Part I (as of 2026-08-25):** Validation unit = layered (participant-level, not merely character-level). Specialists must retain meaningful comparative advantage without a hard quality ceiling. Multi-accounting is in-scope and adversarial. Provisional non-numeric functional shapes may be defined for simulation. **Still forbidden:** promoting Architecture C (or any M1–M8 mechanism) to LOCKED; hard factory-quality ceiling; account-level restrictions; numeric balance values; resolving OQ-001, OQ-002, OQ-003, OQ-004, OQ-005, OQ-012 (implementation), or OQ-017 as canon.

## Part II — HD-EIC-05 to HD-EIC-08
**Source:** `EIC_Human_Rulings_Architecture_C_Falsification_2026-08-25.md` · **Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Responds to:** `EIC_Human_Validation_Gate_Architecture_C_Falsification_v0.1.md`, following the completed α FAIL / β FAIL / γ′ FAIL adversarial evidence cycle (produced under the permissions granted in Part I, specifically HD-EIC-04).
**Ruling context (per GR-004; reconstructed from the source document's own "Controlling prior documents"):** `EIC_Human_Validation_Gate_Architecture_C_Falsification_v0.1.md`, `EIC_Targeted_γ_Prime_Adversarial_Retest_Results_v0.1.md`, `EIC_Comparative_Simulation_Results_v0.1.md`, `EIC_Human_Rulings_2026-08-25.md` (HD-EIC-01–04), `Master_GDD_v1.1.1_Status_Patch.md`, `Authority_Provenance_Reconciliation_Matrix.md`. *(Proxy only.)*

### HD-EIC-05 — Accept / Reject the Falsification
**Decision: ACCEPT FALSIFICATION.**
Architecture C, as currently expressible through the tested Functional Shapes (including the Targeted γ Closure v0.2 refinements and the γ′ variants), has failed to demonstrate the required independent economic-participant comparative advantage under the adversarial conditions authorised by HD-EIC-01–04.
**Architecture C is retired as the leading EIC architecture.**
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED. **Implementation status:** Architecture C is no longer the leading hypothesis.

### HD-EIC-06 — Status of the EIC Requirement Itself
**Decision: REQUIREMENT UNCHANGED.**
The canonical requirement remains binding: *"Important professions should depend upon one another, while interdependence must create opportunity rather than coercive inconvenience"* (PIL-003 lineage). The participant-layer interpretation established by HD-EIC-01 remains in force. Multi-account and organisational internalisation continue to be in-scope adversarial conditions. **The failure of Architecture C does not revise, narrow, or relax the interdependence pillar.**
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED (requirement confirmation).

### HD-EIC-07 — Authorise What Kind of Next Search
**Decision: A — BOUNDED NEW SHAPING, with human-defined structural boundaries.**
A new architecture may be explored. **Automatic generation of an "Architecture D" is not authorised.** Any new shaping effort is permitted only within structural boundaries that the human explicitly defines. **Until those boundaries are stated, no new architectural search or residual-channel invention is authorised.**
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED (authorisation + constraint). **Implementation status:** Bounded new shaping is authorised in principle; the bounding structural commitments must still be supplied by the human before work begins. **⚠ This is the current outstanding gate — see the Open Questions Register.**

### HD-EIC-08 — Confirm Governance Boundary
**Decision: GOVERNANCE BOUNDARIES CONFIRMED.**

| Boundary | Status |

| Numeric tuning | **NOT AUTHORISED** |
| Provenance & Reputation Engine | **DEFERRED** |
| Master GDD consolidation or rewrite | **NOT AUTHORISED** |
| Automatic Architecture D | **NOT AUTHORISED** |
| New residual-channel invention by LLM without human structural boundaries | **NOT AUTHORISED** |
| Prototype evidence reconciliation | Remains a **separate evidence track** |

**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED (boundary confirmation).

**Cumulative effect of Part II (as of 2026-08-25):** Architecture C is retired. PIL-003 and the participant-layer validation unit remain binding and unrevised. Bounded new shaping is authorised **in principle only** — no new EIC architectural search, residual invention, or Architecture D may begin until the human supplies the specific structural boundaries HD-EIC-07 requires. **Confirmed 2026-08-25 (this session), reflecting the project owner's prior decision:**

### WRLD-001 — Persistent Shared World
**Question posed:** "Do you explicitly want the game to be a persistent shared world rather than, for example, a session-based or instanced experience?"
**Decision: CONFIRM.**
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED.

### PLR-001 — Persistent Character Identity
**Question posed:** "Do you explicitly want a player's character to persist as an ongoing identity in the world?" — scoped narrowly to whether persistent character identity itself is a requirement.
**Decision: CONFIRM.**
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED.

### PLR-003 — Persistent Meaningful Ownership
**Meaning as clarified at time of decision:** Players can possess things that meaningfully persist as owned assets in the world (examples could eventually include property, structures, businesses, equipment, etc.).
**Explicitly does not decide:** how ownership works — implementation is separate and remains open.
**Decision: CONFIRM** — "meaningful persistent ownership is locked, but its implementation is not."
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED (principle only).

### CRFT-001 — Differentiated Products
**Meaning as clarified at time of decision:** Crafting should produce products that can meaningfully differ, rather than the economy reducing everything to identical commodities.
**Explicitly does not decide:** how quality works; how experimentation works; whether Exceptional exists; what attributes products have; how factories work; how many tiers exist. All of these remain open (see `Open_Questions_Register.md`, OQ-003, OQ-004, OQ-005).
**Decision: CONFIRM.**
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED (principle only).

### SAFE-001 — Ownership and Transfer Safeguards
**Meaning as clarified at time of decision:** The game needs mechanisms that protect ownership and transfers from unacceptable exploitation or ambiguity.
**Explicitly does not decide:** the mechanism itself — implementation remains open.
**Decision: CONFIRM.**
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED (principle only).
