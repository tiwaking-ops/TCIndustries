# TCIndustries — Human Rulings Register

**Purpose of this document:** A single, chronologically ordered, cumulative record of every explicit human ruling issued for the TCIndustries project to date, across both the EIC programme and GDD-level authority questions. This document **merges** the content of three source rulings — two EIC ruling documents (Section A) and one recovered-and-newly-recorded GDD ruling (Section B) — for single-document readability.

**Authority:** This register carries the same HUMAN-LOCKED authority as its source rulings, to the exact extent stated in each entry below — no more, no less. Nothing in this register expands, reinterprets, or extends any ruling beyond what its source states.

**Provenance guarantee:** The two EIC-programme source documents remain preserved, verbatim and unedited, in archive (`/archive/human_rulings_source/`) as the permanent source of truth. **On any conflict between this register and an original ruling document, the original document governs.** Section B (HD-GDD-01) has no equivalent standalone source document — see its provenance note — so for that ruling, **this register is itself the primary record.**

**Governance note (restated identically from both EIC source documents):** Per GR-001 and project authority rules, these statements are HUMAN-LOCKED only to the extent of the decisions stated below. Downstream mechanical implementations remain PROPOSED or TBD unless and until separately ruled. Canonical presence in this register does not itself constitute human approval of anything beyond what is explicitly recorded.

**Ruling Context Disclosure (per GR-004 — see `Manifest.md` for full text):** Every ruling below now carries a `Ruling context` line stating which documents the producing LLM session had available. Where the original source document already lists a "Controlling References" section, that is used as the best available proxy and is noted as such — it was not originally collected for this purpose.

**Source:** `EIC_Human_Rulings_2026-08-25.md` · **Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Responds to:** `EIC_Human_Decision_Brief_v0.1.md` (Gates 1–4)

### HD-EIC-01 — Layered Unit of Interdependence
**Decision: APPROVE D — Layered.**
Character specialisation remains an intended character-level design property. EIC validation ultimately concerns economically meaningful comparative advantage among independent economic participants, including legitimate multi-account and organisational actors.
**Explicitly does not determine:** the implementation of specialisation (formal budget, soft pressure, or other instruments).
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED (unit of validation only). **Implementation status:** Open / PROPOSED alternatives remain available.

### HD-EIC-02 — Specialist Comparative Value
**Decision: APPROVE hierarchical comparative-value principle.**
Specialists must retain economically meaningful comparative advantages arising from expertise, information, experimentation, discovery, responsiveness, customisation, production economics, reputation, provenance, or related player-created value.
**Explicitly does not authorise:** any permanent hard factory-quality ceiling.
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED (principle only). **Implementation status:** PROPOSED / TBD; no specific residual channel is elevated to LOCKED.

### HD-EIC-03 — Multi-Accounting
**Decision: APPROVE M1.**
Legitimate multi-accounting is an in-scope adversarial condition for EIC validation. The system is not required to equalise multi-account and independent-player economics, but multi-account internalisation must not trivially eliminate economically meaningful independent participation.
**Explicitly does not authorise:** any account-level restriction.
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED (validation stance only). **Implementation status:** Open.

### HD-EIC-04 — Functional Shapes
**Decision: APPROVE.**
The EIC team may define provisional non-numeric functional shapes necessary for simulation. These remain PROPOSED / simulation assumptions, not canonical mechanics.
**Explicitly does not authorise:** any numeric balance values.
**Status:** HUMAN-LOCKED (methodological permission only).

**Cumulative effect of Part I (as of 2026-08-25):** Validation unit = layered (participant-level, not merely character-level). Specialists must retain meaningful comparative advantage without a hard quality ceiling. Multi-accounting is in-scope and adversarial. Provisional non-numeric functional shapes may be defined for simulation. **Still forbidden:** promoting Architecture C (or any M1–M8 mechanism) to LOCKED; hard factory-quality ceiling; account-level restrictions; numeric balance values; resolving OQ-001, OQ-002, OQ-003, OQ-004, OQ-005, OQ-012 (implementation), or OQ-017 as canon.

## HD-ITM-02 — OQ-010 Item Damage, Repair & Destruction Elaboration

baseline decay" determination. Decision record:

   usage-decay system that was supposed to exist never worked; item decay was
   never solved. Item decay is deferred — "we can deal with item decay later."
2. **DEATH DAMAGE:** when a player dies while holding an item, the item takes
   1% of its max durability damage. 1% and the death-trigger are
   owner-authorized values, historically grounded in Pre-CU SWG (items damaged
   ~1% on death while holding them). This is a discrete hit, not a decay
   system.
3. **REPAIR:** items are repairable (repair mechanics, cost, stations, and
   profession structure remain open — SERV-002 / crafting / facility surfaces;
   no repair mechanic specified this session beyond repairability).

   a different behaviour is later ruled.

   "destroy item" menu option. Death-damage does not destroy items. When an
   item is destroyed via the menu, it is removed from the world.
6. **SALVAGE:** no salvage system for item destruction this session — straight
   removal. Reason: Pre-CU SWG did not have a salvage system for items; a
   salvage system may be added later. For now, simplicity.
