**Purpose of this document:** Propose a narrow interpretive clarification of what constitutes EIC validation success/failure, for explicit human approval or amendment, before the Comparative Simulation Specification v0.1.1 is executed against Packages α, β, γ.

**Why this is needed now:** The Simulation Specification v0.1.1 (§7, Decision Rules) already defines SURVIVES/FAILS in terms consistent with the interpretation below, but that language was authored as part of a specification, not ruled on as an explicit human decision. Locking the interpretation before execution avoids a later dispute over whether the pass/fail criterion itself was legitimate.

---

# 1. What This Ruling Would Establish

**Proposed decision:**

Legitimate multi-accounting, multi-boxing, multiple characters, broad profession capability, organisational pooling, and vertical integration are permitted capabilities within TCIndustries. Their existence, or a participant's capacity to use them, is **not by itself** an EIC failure condition.

The relevant EIC failure condition is **not** "a sufficiently capable player or organisation can internalise a production chain."

The relevant EIC validation condition is **whether, despite such capability, independent economic participants retain meaningful comparative advantage and economically attractive opportunities to specialise, trade, cooperate, or provide services to one another, voluntarily.**

Accordingly:

- Internalisation (by a multi-account unit or an organisation) is **evidence relevant to the test**, not sufficient by itself to establish EIC failure.
- A functional-shape package should be judged to **FAIL** only where the authorised adversarial conditions (multi-account internalisation, organisational pooling, manufacturing quality parity, NPC substitution, demand neutralisation) cause independent-participant comparative advantage / voluntary economic opportunity to **collapse**, as measured by the metrics already defined in the governing simulation specification.

---

# 2. What This Ruling Would NOT Do

This ruling, if approved, would **not**:

- Create, select, or imply any new mechanism, residual channel, specialisation-budget form, or manufacturing rule.
- Set any numeric value, threshold, or parameter.
- Define or redefine how ETA, ICR, or any other metric is computed. Measurement definitions remain the responsibility of the governing simulation specification (currently v0.1.1), not this ruling.
- Restrict accounts, characters, professions, or organisations in any way.
- Resolve HD-1 through HD-9 of the earlier Candidate document, or any other open question (OQ-series) not directly addressed above.
- Promote Architecture C, any package (α/β/γ), or any mechanism to LOCKED or canonical status.
- Predetermine the outcome of the α/β/γ execution. It only fixes what counts as a legitimate failure finding if and when that execution occurs.

# 3. Relationship to Existing Canon

This ruling does not conflict with existing HUMAN-LOCKED material; it clarifies how two already-LOCKED/ruled principles interact under adversarial testing:

| Existing authority | Relevance |

| PIL-003 (LOCKED) — Interdependence must create opportunity, not coercive inconvenience; no major profession fully self-sufficient at high effectiveness | This ruling clarifies that "self-sufficient" is evaluated at the level of preserved comparative advantage/opportunity, not at the level of whether internalisation is technically achievable by a sufficiently resourced actor |

| HD-EIC-01 (HUMAN-LOCKED, 2026-08-25) — Validation unit is layered; binding proof is comparative advantage among independent economic participants, including multi-account and organisational actors | This ruling is a direct, narrow elaboration of HD-EIC-01's existing "comparative advantage" language, not a new validation unit |
| HD-EIC-03 (HUMAN-LOCKED, 2026-08-25) — Legitimate multi-accounting is in-scope; system not required to equalise multi-account and independent-player economics, but must not trivially eliminate meaningful independent participation | This ruling gives explicit shape to what "trivially eliminate meaningful independent participation" means for pass/fail purposes: collapse of comparative advantage/opportunity, not mere existence of internalisation capability |

No prior ruling is reversed. No prior ruling's implementation status changes.
