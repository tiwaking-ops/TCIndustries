## HD-EIC-09 — Decision: ADOPT the broad-capability interpretation

Legitimate multi-accounting, multi-boxing, multiple characters, broad profession ownership, and organisational vertical integration are permitted and are **not by themselves failures of PIL-003**. The EIC requirement is concerned with whether meaningful comparative advantage and economically valuable interdependence and opportunity can persist **despite** that capability.

The relevant failure condition is **not** "a sufficiently organised player can perform the entire production chain." It is: "even though a participant can become broadly capable and vertically integrated, the system fails to preserve meaningful comparative advantage and economically attractive reasons for independent participants to specialise, trade, cooperate, or provide services to one another."

**Explicitly does not authorise (unchanged constraints):**
- Character-count or account restrictions.
- Mutually exclusive professions, or required economic dependence.
- Treating successful vertical integration by itself as falsification.
- New architecture generation; new residual channels; numeric tuning; Provenance & Reputation work.
