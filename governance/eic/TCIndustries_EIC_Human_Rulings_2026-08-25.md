# 1. Rulings

## HD-EIC-01 — Layered Unit of Interdependence

**Decision: APPROVE D — Layered.**

Character specialisation remains an intended character-level design property.  

However, EIC validation ultimately concerns economically meaningful comparative advantage among independent economic participants, including legitimate multi-account and organisational actors.

**Explicitly does not determine:**  
The implementation of specialisation (formal budget, soft pressure, or other instruments).

**Status of this ruling:** HUMAN-LOCKED (unit of validation only).  
**Implementation status:** Open / PROPOSED alternatives remain available.

---

## HD-EIC-02 — Specialist Comparative Value

**Decision: APPROVE hierarchical comparative-value principle.**

Specialists must retain economically meaningful comparative advantages that can arise from expertise, information, experimentation, discovery, responsiveness, customisation, production economics, reputation, provenance, or related player-created value.

**Explicitly does not authorise:**  
Any permanent hard factory-quality ceiling.

**Implementation status:** PROPOSED / TBD.  
No specific residual channel is elevated to LOCKED by this ruling. The principle is that economically meaningful comparative advantage must remain possible without reliance on an arbitrary hard quality lock.

**Status of this ruling:** HUMAN-LOCKED (principle only).

---

## HD-EIC-03 — Multi-Accounting

**Decision: APPROVE M1.**

Legitimate multi-accounting is an in-scope adversarial condition for EIC validation.

The system is not required to equalise multi-account and independent-player economics, but multi-account internalisation must not trivially eliminate economically meaningful independent participation.

**Explicitly does not authorise:**  
Any account-level restriction.

**Status of this ruling:** HUMAN-LOCKED (validation stance only).  
**Implementation status:** Open. No anti-multi-account instrument is authorised by this ruling.

---

## HD-EIC-04 — Functional Shapes

**Decision: APPROVE.**

The EIC team may define provisional non-numeric functional shapes necessary for simulation.

These are:  
**PROPOSED / simulation assumptions**, not canonical mechanics.

**Explicitly does not authorise:**  
Any numeric balance values.

**Status of this ruling:** HUMAN-LOCKED (methodological permission only).
