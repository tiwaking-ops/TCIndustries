## HD-EIC-05 — Disposition of Architecture C Evidence

**Decision: ACCEPT FALSIFICATION.**

The adversarial evidence (Packages α, β, γ′-1, γ′-2 under the Phase 0–6 sequence of the Comparative Simulation Specification v0.1.1) is accepted as showing that no tested coherent package preserved independent-participant comparative advantage (positive ETA on a demand-valued residual channel) under combined multi-account, organisational, manufacturing-parity, NPC-substitution, and demand-neutralisation closure.

**Status of this ruling:** HUMAN-LOCKED (acceptance of evidence and its consequence for Architecture C).

**Explicitly established:**
Architecture C, as expressed through the shape packages tested, is **retired as the leading hypothesis**. It is falsified in its current form, not merely inconclusive.

**Explicitly not established:**
- That no coherent architecture can satisfy PIL-003 (see HD-EIC-06).
- Any specific cause of failure as load-bearing versus incidental (that remains for future analysis if pursued).
- Any replacement mechanism.
