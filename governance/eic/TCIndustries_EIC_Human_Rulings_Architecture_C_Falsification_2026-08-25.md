**Decision: ACCEPT FALSIFICATION**

Architecture C, as currently expressible through the tested Functional Shapes (including the Targeted γ Closure v0.2 refinements and the γ′ variants), has failed to demonstrate the required independent economic-participant comparative advantage under the adversarial conditions authorised by HD-EIC-01–04.

Architecture C is retired as the leading EIC architecture.

**Status of this ruling:** HUMAN-LOCKED.  
**Implementation status:** Architecture C is no longer the leading hypothesis.

## HD-EIC-06 — Status of the EIC Requirement Itself

**Decision: REQUIREMENT UNCHANGED**

The canonical requirement remains binding:

> Important professions should depend upon one another, while interdependence must create opportunity rather than coercive inconvenience. (PIL-003 lineage)

The participant-layer interpretation established by HD-EIC-01 remains in force. Multi-account and organisational internalisation continue to be in-scope adversarial conditions.

The failure of Architecture C does not revise, narrow, or relax the interdependence pillar.

**Status of this ruling:** HUMAN-LOCKED (requirement confirmation).  
**Implementation status:** The requirement continues to constrain any future architecture.

# 4. Immediate Next Requirement

Before any new EIC architectural work may commence, the human must supply the structural boundaries referenced in HD-EIC-07.

These boundaries answer the question left open by the falsification:

> What structural source(s) of economic comparative advantage is the project actually willing to make a durable design commitment to?

Until that statement exists, the LLM is not authorised to invent residuals, propose Architecture D, or reopen the simulation loop.

Possible forms the boundary statement might take (illustrative only; not proposals):

- Explicit acceptance that certain forms of scarcity, information asymmetry, or demand differentiation will be treated as durable design commitments.  
- Explicit rejection of pure soft-residual approaches that organisations can fully internalise.  
- Any other human-defined constraint that bounds the next search.

The human may also choose to pause and supply boundaries later. In that case the programme remains at the post-falsification gate with no further EIC architectural activity.
