# 4. Package γ — Facility / Information Concentration + Strong Discovery Residuals

**Composition (unchanged):**  
S4 + S3 | R3 + R4 + R5 | C1 + C4 | Mfg3 + Mfg4 | Same NPC baseline | Elevated situational + organisational + discovery-linked demand.

### Phase-by-phase structural results

| Phase | ETA (independent specialist) | ICR (multi-account / org) | Residual channels still generating advantage | Notes |
|-------|------------------------------|---------------------------|----------------------------------------------|-------|
| 0 Baseline | Positive | Partial | Discovery-linked attributes, process knowledge, regional/temporal information, facility-constrained production | |
| 1 Factory Quality Parity | Positive on information and discovery residuals | Rising | Process knowledge, discovery, regional information remain | Throughput vs flexibility (Mfg3) preserves some specialist edge on novel or difficult recipes |
| 2 Multi-account Internalisation | Reduced but not eliminated | High | Information maintenance (S3) and facility opportunity cost (S4) still apply at participant layer; multi-account can mitigate but not fully erase discovery/information advantage if knowledge is actively maintained and location-sensitive | Stronger than α or β on this phase |
| 3 Organisation + Capital | Contested | High but incomplete | Organisations can own facilities (S4 vulnerability) and share process knowledge; however, ongoing discovery (R3) and regional/temporal variation (R5) create continuing information asymmetry that is costly to fully internalise across all regions and recipes | Key open question: how costly is full organisational coverage of discovery space? |
| 4 NPC Substitution | Contested | High | Discovery-linked and regional information residuals hold longest against NPCs (NPCs are static by construction in the package); process knowledge can be partially captured | |
| 5 Demand Neutralisation | Sensitive | Rising toward closure | If discovery-linked and situational demand weight remains, advantage persists; if those weights are reduced, advantage erodes | Package is more dependent on specific demand composition than α |
| **6 Combined Closure** | **Cannot be determined with current shaping** | **Cannot be determined** | Discovery/process knowledge and regional information are the only candidates; their economic magnitude under full organisational + multi-account + demand pressure is under-specified | |

**Judgment for Package γ:** **INCONCLUSIVE** (leans FAIL under present specification)

Package γ is the strongest of the three against early internalisation because concentration pressure sits at facilities and information rather than pure character skill. However, two critical relationships remain under-specified in the Functional Shapes:

1. The real opportunity cost and coverage cost for an organisation to internalise ongoing discovery and regional information across the relevant production domains.  
2. The actual demand weight that continues to attach to discovery-linked and situationally optimal goods once average production capability rises.

Without those relationships being shaped more tightly, Phase 6 cannot be resolved. The package cannot be declared to SURVIVE. It also cannot yet be declared to FAIL with the same confidence as α and β. The honest result is INCONCLUSIVE with a structural lean toward failure if organisational information-sharing and facility ownership prove cheap relative to the gains.

# 6. Under-Specification Report

The experiment could not fully discriminate Package γ (and therefore could not produce a clean Architecture-level FAIL for every coherent package) because the following relationships remain under-specified in Provisional Functional Shapes v0.1:

1. **Magnitude and form of facility opportunity cost (S4)** after organisational ownership is allowed.  
2. **Cost and completeness of organisational internalisation of ongoing discovery and regional/temporal information (R3 + R5 + S3).**  
3. **Demand elasticity / residual weight** on discovery-linked, situational, and process-novelty goods once average production capability and organisational supply rise.  
4. **Degree to which process knowledge can be recorded, sold, or replicated** without ongoing specialist involvement.

These are not missing residual *channels*; they are missing quantitative or structural relationships that determine whether the channels that do exist remain economically meaningful under optimiser pressure. Filling them with invented assumptions would violate the experimental rules. Therefore INCONCLUSIVE is the correct result for γ.

No other packages were blocked by under-specification; α and β fail on structural grounds already present in the shapes.

# 7. Architecture-Level Conclusion (Mandatory Statement)

Under the tested packages, independent economic participants **did not** retain demonstrably meaningful comparative advantage after legitimate internalisation, manufacturing quality parity, NPC substitution, and demand neutralisation.

The load-bearing residual channel(s), if any, were: **none proven**. Discovery-linked process knowledge and regional/temporal information remain the only candidates still open, but only inside Package γ and only because their economic weight is under-specified.

**Architecture C as currently shaped is weakened / not supported by this evidence.**

Unresolved dimensions that still prevent full discrimination: facility opportunity-cost magnitude under organisational ownership; cost of covering ongoing discovery space; residual demand weight on novelty and situational optimisation.
